# Core Style Prompt (English)

## A. Reference-faithful version

High-quality Japanese anime fantasy character illustration, refined semi-realistic anime rendering, a blend of soft digital painting and delicate gradient shading. Elegant naturalized anime anatomy, finely rendered blue eyes, soft facial shadows and subtle blush. Long layered hair in deep navy, indigo, blue-violet and lavender tones, combining broad coherent hair locks with a controlled amount of fine flyaway strands and smooth flowing highlights.

Delicate, clean linework, carefully rendered skin, and clearly differentiated materials for lace, sheer fabric, ribbons and cloth. A dim blue-violet environment with cool ambient illumination, restrained local highlights and soft cool rim light. Shallow depth of field, gentle bokeh, a few subtle bubbles and floating points of light. Dreamlike, quiet, polished atmosphere, with the character remaining the clear focal point.

## B. Soft natural adaptation (recommended)

Refined Japanese semi-realistic anime fantasy illustration, soft painterly digital rendering, smooth delicate linework, naturalized character anatomy, clear but not exaggerated blue eyes, gentle skin gradients and subtle blush. Long blue-to-blue-violet hair with coherent layered locks and controlled fine strand detail.

A restrained palette of muted blue, blue-gray, lavender, pearl white and scene-appropriate complementary colors. Moderate-to-low contrast, soft tonal transitions, no blown highlights or aggressive rim lighting. Distinct and believable material rendering for skin, hair, lace, knitwear, leather and translucent fabric. A clear light direction, physically coherent shadows and environmental bounce light, with moderate atmospheric depth and depth of field. Refined, calm and natural, without unnecessary decorative clutter.

## C. Negative constraints

Avoid heavy black outlines, flat cel shading, plastic skin, excessive sharpening, harsh specular highlights, blown-out rim light, excessive bloom, globally oversaturated blue-violet filters, excessive contrast, chaotic hair strands, too many fabric wrinkles, overly dense lace, repetitive ornaments, distracting backgrounds, malformed hands, incorrect anatomy, intersecting body parts, incorrectly attached tails or fins, text, watermark, collage, multi-panel layout, character sheet.

## D. How to use

Use either A or B as the style block, then specify the following independently for every illustration:
- character identity and distinguishing features;
- garment design and construction;
- pose, balance and center of gravity;
- facial expression and gaze direction;
- setting, time and weather;
- camera distance, angle and composition;
- light source direction, softness and color temperature.

Do not rely on “the same character in a different outfit” alone. Explicitly redesign the pose, expression, camera and environment to prevent the model from copying the reference composition.
