## 4. Lighting and material modules

### Existing confirmed style — reuse, do not duplicate

`STYLE-WARM-WINDOW@v1.0` already exists in the project materials. It defines warm-gold side / back window light, grey-olive and warm-grey shadows, fine dark-brown-grey linework, soft cel-shading plus painterly gradients, localized bloom, and photographic depth of field. Keep it as the confirmed style card.

### New candidate 1 — lantern point-light night

Status: `draft_pending_confirmation`.

- One nearby kerosene lantern is the main warm key light.
- Light intensity falls off quickly with distance; the lit region is local rather than uniform.
- Cool moonlight is a weak ambient / rim fill, not a second broad key.
- Wet rock, water, fish scales, metal cookware and droplets catch small localized highlights.
- Deep blue shadows retain detail.
- During cooking, the stove flame may add a small local secondary source.

### New candidate 2 — noon coastal directional light

Status: `draft_pending_confirmation` as a reusable style module; it is already a user-directed SHOT requirement for the sports sequence.

- High midday sun is the primary directional source.
- Use visible cast shadows with a consistent direction and contact to ground / rocks.
- Sea and sky provide cool reflected fill, but must not erase the key-light direction.
- Keep skin, wet surfaces and saltwater droplets with localized specular highlights.
- Avoid evenly washed, parallel-looking illumination and avoid sunset / golden-hour colour when the scene is specified as noon.

### Material / state module — post-exertion

This belongs to SHOT, not STYLE:

- Sweat / water: forehead, temples, cheeks, neck, shoulders, arms, thighs, knees, shins and feet.
- Dirt / sand / rock dust: mainly skin, knees, lower legs, soles, toes and sandal contact points.
- Clothing remains comparatively clean; allow only plausible small wet edges or contact marks.
- For the post-exertion tide-pool scene, the jacket is removed; retain the sports top and functional shorts.
- Expressions may show concentration, exertion, relief or rest. The character need not look at the camera.
